## Community post

Title: We reviewed 45 sweepstakes profiles—here are the warning signs that repeated

Body:

I help maintain SafeTracker, an independent comparison project for sweepstakes sites and promotions. We reviewed 29 recurring platforms and 16 currently open promotions using the same five-part ScamFactor methodology.

The repeated issues were not limited to obviously fake sites:

- legitimate brands being impersonated in prize notices;
- directories sending users to outside sponsors with different privacy terms;
- open promotions being presented without completed fulfillment evidence;
- persistent marketing and data collection;
- unclear distinctions between a site’s own prizes and prizes it merely lists.

The full report includes the score distribution, scope, methodology, and links to the underlying profiles:
`https://sweeps.safetrackerhub.com/guides/2026-sweepstakes-scam-warning-signs-report?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=scam-report-launch`

I am interested in factual corrections and additional primary sources. This is not a request to brigade, mass-enter promotions, or treat any score as a guarantee.

## Sponsor-readiness scorecard

- 1,000 monthly sessions
- 500 monthly organic sessions
- 100 monthly tracked outbound clicks
- 100 newsletter subscribers
- 3 genuine referring domains
- A campaign report showing which pages and operators generated clicks

Before these thresholds, describe paid opportunities as a transparent founding-sponsor or pilot program rather than established inventory.
