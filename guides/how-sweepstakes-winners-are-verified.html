How Sweepstakes Winners Are Verified

Winning is usually a process, not a single congratulatory message. Sponsors may need to confirm the selected entrant’s identity, eligibility, compliance with the rules, and tax information before formally awarding a prize.

Save this checklist

Quick check

  • Match the notice to a promotion you entered and its official rules.
  • Verify the sender using contact information published by the sponsor.
  • Check the response deadline and required documents carefully.
  • Never pay a fee or purchase gift cards to complete verification.

Selection is followed by eligibility review

A potential winner may be selected randomly or according to the judging method described in the rules. The sponsor or administrator then confirms that the entry was valid and that the person meets age, location, timing, and other eligibility requirements.

If the selected entrant cannot be reached, misses a deadline, or is ineligible, the rules may allow an alternate winner to be selected.

Common verification documents

Depending on the prize, a winner may receive an affidavit of eligibility, liability or publicity release, tax form, or identity-verification request. These documents are not automatically suspicious, but they should arrive through a channel you have independently confirmed.

Read the entire document and compare it with the official rules before signing. Ask the administrator to explain unfamiliar terms.

  • Full legal name, age, and mailing address.
  • Proof of eligibility or identity.
  • A signed affidavit or release.
  • Taxpayer information for reportable prizes.
  • Travel-companion forms for trip prizes.

Confirm the award before sharing sensitive data

Search the sponsor’s official website independently and contact the listed administrator. Do not rely solely on a reply address, direct message, or phone number supplied in the notification.

Keep copies of the rules, notice, correspondence, signed forms, and delivery records. These records help with disputes, valuation, and taxes.
